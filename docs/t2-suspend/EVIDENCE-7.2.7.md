# Hardware record: T2 hibernation on linux-t2 7.2.7

This is the record of every live gate that requalified hibernation on the MacBookAir9,1 after linux-t2 moved from 7.2.6 to 7.2.7, plus what is not proven. It was compiled on 2026-09-30 from the operator logs and scripts under `~/.local/state/codex-mba-autonomous/gen-7.2.7/`, the root-owned product ledger and generation trial ledger (read with read-only `sudo`), `journalctl --list-boots`, the local handoff file and the repository history. Times are local (EDT). The procedure that produced it is [REQUALIFICATION.md](REQUALIFICATION.md); the map of documents is [README.md](README.md). The record is for the exact tested machine and kernel. A successful row is evidence for that row, not a general claim.

## What is not proven

Battery-only S4 and S3 on the source image, both listed here earlier, were closed on 2026-09-30 by cycle `edb3bf90` and the 13:41 S3 row above.

- **Other T2 models.** Only the MacBookAir9,1 (board `J230kAP`) was tested. The guard matches that model explicitly.
- **Low-reserve behaviour and long soak.** Three successful routine cycles on 7.2.7 (one on battery) are not a reliability measurement. No repeated-cycle, long-idle or power-loss-during-write soak exists.
- **The interactive `omarchy update` prompts on hardware.** The 7.2.7 update was run by hand under maintenance; the pre and post hook has only shell-test coverage.
- **Unvetoed kernel routes.** Suspend-then-hibernate, hybrid-sleep and direct `/sys/power/state` writes are outside the marker vetoes; the guard's image check is the backstop ([RESUME.md](RESUME.md), next tasks).
- **Hibernation on a stock kernel with upstream drivers.** Not attempted; see the upstream path in [HIBERNATION-OVERVIEW.md](HIBERNATION-OVERVIEW.md).

logind records a reboot requested at 12:56:55 from session-6.scope (owner-initiated from another session); the idle lock engaged 12:56:24 and reported secure 12:56:29; no PAM attempt was logged after the lock; earlier locks at 12:27 and 12:29 on the same boot authenticated normally; cause unknown.
